How the course works

One decoding routine.

Each lesson recycles old code before adding a small new layer. The child practises the same reliable reading process with gradually richer words and short texts.

1 · HEARclean phoneme
2 · MAPsound ↔ spelling
3 · BLENDleft to right
4 · READword + sentence
5 · MEANINGcheck sense

Best fit: a child who already recognises most letters but reads slowly, guesses, or cannot blend confidently. If basic letter recognition is still insecure, add a short foundation sound phase before Mission 1.

Important: this is a compact decoding course, not a promise to master every English spelling in four hours. The goal is a strong strategy for simple words, frequent patterns and short Pre‑A1 texts.
